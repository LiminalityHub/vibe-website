# Product

## Platform
web

## Users
English-speaking prospective Vibe iPhone users and people seeking support or privacy information.

## Product Purpose
Introduce the app honestly, help users resolve common issues, and make the pre-launch privacy draft readable.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three static pages with no backend, analytics, forms or runtime dependencies. GitHub Pages deployment is prepared, not performed. Contact and legal identity details remain explicit placeholders. No live App Store listing is assumed.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the selected chrome-hands mark and red #CC0000. Use bold system typography per the approved brief. Photography is existing synthetic illustration, never presented as customer work or app screenshots.
