Common questions

How do I add my photos?

Choose a layout, then use Add photos in the editor to open Apple’s photo picker. Select the images you want to use and place them in your layout. Vibe receives the images you choose; this flow does not request access to browse your entire photo library.

Why won’t my slides save to Photos?

Vibe asks for permission to add images to Photos when you save. If access was denied, open iPhone Settings, find Vibe under Apps, and review its Photos permission. Allow adding photos if that option is shown, then try again. Also check that your device has enough free storage.

Where are my projects saved?

Saved projects live locally in Vibe on your iPhone. Find them in Projects to continue editing. Vibe does not provide account-based project sync. Keep exported copies of important work; deleting the app can remove its locally stored projects.

Can I remove a project or photo?

Delete a project from Projects, using its delete action or selection mode. This removes the project’s local copy, not the original images in your photo library. Slides already saved to Photos are separate copies; delete those in Photos if you no longer want them.

What does Vibe PRO unlock?

PRO gives access to premium layouts, stickers and fonts, and supports projects with up to 20 slides. Free projects support up to 10 slides. Check the in-app offer for the current price and any trial eligibility; pricing and availability are supplied by the App Store.

I subscribed. How do I restore access?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open a Vibe PRO offer screen and tap Restore Purchases. An active subscription is required. If access has not returned, check your subscription status in iPhone Settings and try again with an internet connection.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select Vibe to review or cancel your subscription. Removing Vibe from your phone does not cancel a subscription. Read Apple’s subscription instructions.

What happens if PRO expires?

Your projects remain editable and available to preview. Exporting projects with more than 10 slides, or with premium content, requires active PRO access. You can also remove premium content and bring the project within the free limits.

Does Vibe upload my photos?

The current app edits and stores project photos on your device. It does not upload those photos to Vibe’s content servers. It connects to download layouts and artwork and to check PRO access. Read the draft privacy policy for the details.
