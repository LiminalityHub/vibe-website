Privacy at a glance

  • Your creations: selected photos, edited text, layouts and project thumbnails are processed and saved on your iPhone. The current app does not upload your project photos to Vibe’s servers.
  • Online services: the app downloads templates and stickers through Cloudflare. PRO access checks send Apple-signed transaction information to Vibe’s broker and Apple.
  • Analytics: no remote analytics endpoint is enabled in the inspected app configuration. Local event logging and a local installation identifier exist.
  • This website: no added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub Pages may process technical visitor information to host and secure the site.
  • Your choices: choose which photos to import, control Photos saving permission, and delete local projects. Other privacy rights depend on applicable law.
  • Still to confirm: operator, contact details, jurisdictions, provider retention, international transfers and children’s policy.

Information handled by Vibe

Photos and local projects

When you select photos using Apple’s photo picker, Vibe receives the selected image files. Editing takes place on your device. Saved projects contain source images, your text and editing choices, project metadata and a thumbnail. Downloaded templates, stickers and preferences also use local storage. These local files are not an account-based cloud library.

Purchases and content requests

Apple manages App Store purchases and billing. Vibe uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine PRO access. For private content, the app sends the requested template or sticker identifier and version, and an Apple-signed transaction, to a Cloudflare-hosted broker. The broker checks subscription status with Apple and returns short-lived content links. Vibe does not receive your full payment card details through this flow.

Catalogue, preview and artwork downloads involve ordinary network request information, which can include an IP address and request metadata at the hosting provider. The current content requests do not upload your project photos.

Local events and preferences

Vibe creates a local random installation identifier and records event names in the device’s logging system. App settings and onboarding selections are stored locally. No remote analytics endpoint is enabled in the inspected configuration.

Support and website visits

If you contact us once support is available, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. This static website has no contact form or added tracking scripts. GitHub, as the intended website host, may process visitor technical information such as IP addresses and request logs.

How and why information is used

On-device information is used to place and edit your photos, reopen saved projects, preserve your preferences, preview artwork and save finished slides to Photos. Network requests retrieve the catalogue and artwork. Subscription information is used to verify access to paid content. Support information is used to investigate and respond to the issue you describe.

Photo permissions and your control

You choose the images to import through Apple’s photo picker. Saving slides asks for permission to add to Photos. You can review the app’s Photos permission in iPhone Settings. Denying save permission prevents saving to Photos but does not grant Vibe broader access to your library.

You can delete local projects in Vibe. This does not delete the original photos or exported slides from Apple Photos. Removing the app can remove locally stored projects. Device backups, if enabled in your Apple settings, may retain app data under Apple’s backup practices; Vibe does not offer its own project-sync account.

Service providers and sharing

Apple: provides the photo picker, Photos permissions, App Store billing and subscription verification. Apple handles information under its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Cloudflare: hosts the app’s public catalogue and artwork and the PRO authorization broker and private content storage. Requests may expose technical information to Cloudflare. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

GitHub Pages: is the intended host for this website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How long information is kept

Local saved projects remain in app storage until you delete them or the app’s data is removed. Exported slides in Photos are separate files that you manage in Photos. Downloaded content and preferences can persist in app storage; some temporary content may be removed by the app or operating system. Device backups may have a separate lifecycle.

Cookies, analytics and external links

This website does not add cookies, local storage, analytics or advertising scripts. It loads its visual assets locally rather than embedding social widgets or remote fonts. GitHub’s infrastructure may still handle technical visitor information to operate and secure hosting.

The inspected app configuration has no remote telemetry endpoint. If remote analytics is enabled later, its event payload can include an installation identifier, timestamps, template identifiers, format, slide count, export duration and onboarding or purchase context. The current payload excludes image bytes and filenames. This policy must be revised and any required choices implemented before that feature is enabled.

Links to Apple, Cloudflare, GitHub or social services lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for the configured catalogue and broker connections. PRO content requests use verified subscription information and short-lived object links. Project files use the app’s local storage. No system can guarantee complete security.
